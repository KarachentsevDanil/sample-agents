# Walmart: ChatGPT checkout converted 3x worse than website

Captured on: 2026-03-23
HN context: front-page story on Hacker News on 2026-03-23
Source URL: https://searchengineland.com/walmart-chatgpt-checkout-converted-worse-472071

Raw text:

Search Engine Land reports that Walmart tested about 200,000 items through OpenAI Instant Checkout and found that purchases completed inside ChatGPT converted at one-third the rate of click-out purchases on Walmart’s website. Walmart described the in-chat experience as unsatisfying and is moving toward an approach where its own assistant and systems stay in the loop for identity, cart sync, and checkout.

The main signal here is that an agentic surface can work technically and still lose on conversion. The article frames owned merchant environments as stronger than third-party chat surfaces when trust, product context, and purchase flow quality matter.
