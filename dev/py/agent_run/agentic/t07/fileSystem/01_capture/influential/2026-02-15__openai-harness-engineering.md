## Raw notes

- The reported leverage comes from humans designing the environment, constraints, and feedback loops while agents execute.
- As throughput rises, review bandwidth becomes the scarce resource.
- Repository knowledge needs to be structured and inspectable by the agent, not buried in one giant instruction file.
- Mechanical boundaries, custom lints, and structural tests act as speed multipliers in agent-heavy systems.
